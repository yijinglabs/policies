# Privacy Policy — 易经 (I Ching) / 隐私政策

_Last updated / 最后更新: 2026-06-06_

This privacy policy applies to the **易经 (I Ching)** Android app
(`com.hexagramlabs.iching`) ("the App"), published by Hexagram Labs ("we", "us").

## English

### Summary

The App does not require an account and does not collect or transmit your
personal information itself. All hexagram content is bundled in the App and works
offline. The free version shows ads through Google AdMob, which collects some
data as described below.

### Information the App stores on your device

The App stores only a local **"remove ads" purchase flag** to remember whether
you have removed ads. It does not create accounts or upload any data.

### Advertising (free version)

The free version displays a banner ad using **Google AdMob**. To serve ads,
Google may collect and process data such as your device's advertising ID, IP
address, and general device information, in accordance with Google's policies.

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses data from apps using its services:
  https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalization in
**Settings → Privacy → Ads**. The one-time "remove ads" purchase removes the
banner and the ads SDK is not used.

### Purchases

The optional "remove ads" purchase is processed by **Google Play Billing**. We
do not receive or store your payment details.

### Children

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal
information from children.

### Contact

Questions: **yijinglabs@gmail.com**

## 简体中文

### 概述

本应用无需注册账号，自身不收集、不上传您的个人信息。全部卦象内容内置于应用中，可
完全离线使用。免费版通过 Google AdMob 展示广告，相关数据收集见下文。

### 应用在设备上保存的信息

应用仅在本机保存一个“去广告”购买状态标记，用于记住您是否已去除广告；不会创建账号，
也不会上传任何数据。

### 广告（免费版）

免费版使用 **Google AdMob** 展示横幅广告。为投放广告，Google 可能依据其政策收集和
处理设备广告 ID、IP 地址及一般设备信息等数据。

- Google 隐私政策：https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Google 如何使用相关数据：https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

您可在“设置 → 隐私 → 广告”中重置或删除广告 ID 并关闭个性化广告。一次性“去广告”内
购后将不再展示广告，也不再使用广告 SDK。

### 内购

可选的“去广告”内购由 **Google Play 结算**处理，我们不会收到或保存您的支付信息。

### 儿童

本应用并非面向儿童，也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

### 联系方式

如有疑问：**yijinglabs@gmail.com**
