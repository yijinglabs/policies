# Privacy Policy — Timer Series

_Last updated: 2026-06-14_

This privacy policy applies to the **Timer Series** Android app
(`com.hexagramlabs.timerseries`) ("the App"), published by Hexagram Labs ("we", "us").

## Summary

The App does not require an account and does not collect or transmit your
personal information itself. Your timer series, period names, repeat sections,
and any announcements you record stay on your device. The free version shows
ads through Google AdMob, which collects some data as described below.

## Information the App stores on your device

- **Your timer series and settings**, and **any audio announcements you record**,
  are saved only in the App's private storage on your device. They are not
  uploaded to us or to any third party. Uninstalling the App deletes them.
- A **"remove ads" purchase flag** is stored locally to remember whether you have
  removed ads.

## Microphone

If you choose to record a custom announcement for a period, the App uses your
device microphone to create that recording. Recordings are stored locally on
your device and are never transmitted off the device. The microphone is not used
at any other time.

## Advertising (free version)

The free version displays a banner ad using **Google AdMob**. To serve ads,
Google may collect and process data such as your device's advertising ID, IP
address, and general device information, in accordance with Google's policies.

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses information from apps that use its services:
  https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out of ad personalization,
in your device settings under **Settings → Privacy → Ads**. Purchasing the
one-time "remove ads" option removes the banner and the ads SDK is not used.

## Purchases

The optional "remove ads" purchase is processed by **Google Play Billing**. We
do not receive or store your payment details; payment is handled entirely by
Google Play.

## Children

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal
information from children.

## Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by the "Last
updated" date above.
